What is HIPAA?

HIPAA or The Health Insurance Portability Accountability Act, is an act that was enacted in 1996 in the United States to protect sensitive patient health information (otherwise known as PHI).

In this application's case, testing is conducted in a way such that no real patient data is being used. This application is not meant to be used in a real-world scenario, unless it is ironed out.

This also means that the data being used to test in this AI-supported summarizer must be synthetic, and has to comply to HIPAA standards (ie. Patient names get redacted).

Due to the aforementioned circumstances listed above, this means that you are strongly limited to fake patient summaries in order to test for HIPAA compliance, accuracy, and tests to make sure that functionality works accordingly in a way, such that it does not compromise PHI.

All summaries must use fake data during testing to ensure compliance and accuracy.
