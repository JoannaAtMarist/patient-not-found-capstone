What do you need help with?

Welcome!
Thank you for considering Patient Not Found, a Patient Information Summarizer and Redactor program.

1. Getting Started
Before you can create summaries, you must create an account. First, head to the login page. Since you may not have created an account yet, press the “Create Account” link underlined below the login button.

Fill out the required fields in the Account Creation page. Fields include Username (must be unique), First Name, Last Name, Email, and Password. Before clicking “Create Account”, please select a role.

Once you have created an account, you will be redirected to the Login page. Enter your username and password, and press the “Log In” button.

2. Navigation Bar
Have you just created an account? Splendid. Now it's time to get familiarized with the User Interface. Don't worry, it is simple enough for anyone to remember.

On the top left is the Home Button. When you leave the Home Menu, this is your way back to it. To access the Account Settings Menu, click on the Gear icon. If you want to log out of the application, click “Logout”, underlined in black.

3. Home Menu
Depending on your role, you may see two or three big buttons on the Home Menu. For standard Users, you have two buttons: “Create New Summary”, and “Go to Account Settings". Account Settings works the same way as the Account Settings button on the Navigation Bar. The Create New Summary button redirects you to the summary creation page.

4. Administrator Role
As an Administrator, you will see an extra button on the Home Menu. The Admin Dashboard allows you to see who logged into the program.

5. Account Settings
Made a mistake while creating your account? The Account Settings page makes editing your user profile a breeze. Simply click on the credential you need to change, and enter a new value in the field. If you want to save your changes, click “Save and Exit” to save your new credentials. Please note that changing your username and/or password will require you to use those credentials next time you log in.

If you do not want to save any changes you've made to your account, click “Quit Without Saving.”

6. Summary Creation
Now that you've mastered navigating the User Interface, you are now ready to create summaries. To create a summary, simply paste text into the “Paste Text Here” input field. Alternatively, you can upload PDF or DOC files and summarize them that way.

When you're done pasting or uploading files to the page, simply click the “Redact PHI” button. Your summary should begin generating in a few seconds. Once the summary is finished generating, you can read the summary and download it to your computer as a PDF or DOC file. If you are not satisfied with the result, click “Edit Summary” to edit the generated summary.

7. What do “Source Highlights” and “Confidence Flags” mean?
Source Highlights show where the summary came from. The system marks matching text in the original note with a light yellow highlight. This helps you verify that key findings in the summary are supported by the source note.

Confidence Flags call out sentences in the summary that may need a second look. You will see: ✓ = typical confidence and ⚠︎ = needs review. A sentence is flagged when it’s hard to match to the source wording or includes cautious language (e.g., “may,” “likely,” “appears”).

How to use them
• Turn these features on/off with the checkboxes under the summary box (“Source text highlighting” and “Confidence flags”).
• Click into the summary to edit it; the flags/highlights will refresh automatically when you switch tabs or toggle the checkboxes.
• Use highlights to quickly trace the summary back to the note; use flags to decide where to confirm details in the chart or revise wording.

What they are not
• They are assistive cues, not a diagnosis or clinical decision support.
• Highlights are approximate text matches; if the note’s wording is very different, a sentence may not highlight even if it’s clinically correct.
